Effects on Education

Ang teenage pregnancy ay major barrier sa pagtatapos ng pag-aaral.

Ang mga teenage mothers ay less likely na makapag-high school at college graduation kaysa sa mga hindi naging buntis sa teenage years[3].

This limits their future opportunities and earning potential, creating a cycle of poverty that can affect their children too.

Health Risks

Ang mga very young mothers (10-14) ay may higher risk ng:

  • Preterm birth
  • Low birth weight
  • Eclampsia (high blood pressure complications)
  • Obstetric fistula
  • Maternal mortality[3]

Ang mga babies ng teenage mothers din ay may higher risk ng health complications.

Summary

Ang numbers ay nagpapakita na ang teenage pregnancy ay:

  • Declining, pero still a major problem
  • Higher sa rural areas at poorest families
  • Most alarming sa very young adolescents (10-14)
  • May serious effects sa education at health
