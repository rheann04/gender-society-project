Emergency Help

If you are in immediate danger, experiencing abuse, or need urgent help:

  • Call 911 for emergency police response
  • Call 1343 for PNP Women and Children Protection Center (Aleng Pulis): 0920-907-1717[17]
  • Go to the nearest hospital or police station

If You're a Pregnant Teen

Ang pregnancy sa teenage years ay overwhelming, pero may rights ka at may resources na available.

Your Rights:

  • Right to stay in school (RA 9710)[15]
  • Right to health services (RA 10354)[11]
  • Right to privacy
  • Right to non-discrimination

What You Can Do:

  • Go to a health center or hospital for prenatal check-up
  • Talk to a trusted adult (parent, relative, teacher, guidance counselor)
  • Contact POPCOM for information and referral: Regional offices available nationwide
  • If you want to continue school, talk to your school about accommodations

Remember: Pregnancy doesn't mean your life is over. Many teen mothers finish school and build good lives. You can too.

If You're a Teen Parent (Mother or Father)

Parenting is hard sa anumang edad. Mas mahirap sa teenage years, pero possible. May support available.

For Teen Mothers:

  • You can continue your education through regular school or ALS[10]
  • You may qualify for government assistance programs
  • Some schools have day-care or breastfeeding stations (check with your school)

For Teen Fathers:

  • You have financial responsibilities to your child
  • You can seek parenting education and support
  • Being present and responsible makes a big difference

Resources:

  • DSWD WiSUPPORT: Text "DSWD WiSUPPORT Name/Sex/Age/Region/Message" to 0918-912-2813[18]
  • POPCOM: For adolescent health and development information[19]
  • Local Health Centers: For prenatal and postnatal care

If You're a Parent or Teacher

Ang role mo ay crucial sa pag-support sa mga kabataan. Here's what you can do:

For Parents:

  • Create a safe space for open communication
  • Educate yourself about adolescent reproductive health
  • Support your child if they disclose pregnancy — don't shame
  • Know their rights (RA 9710, RA 10354)
  • Access parenting resources from POPCOM and DSWD

For Teachers:

  • Follow DepEd Order No. 31 s. 2018 on CSE implementation[12]
  • Do not expel or discriminate against pregnant students (RA 9710)[15]
  • Refer students to guidance counselors and health services
  • Be a trusted adult they can approach

Resources for Adults:

  • POPCOM: Parent education programs[19]
  • DSWD: Parenting and family support programs[18]
  • DepEd: CSE implementation guidelines[12]

If Someone is Pressuring or Hurting You

⚠️ If you are in immediate danger, call 911 or go to the nearest police station.

If someone is pressuring, abusing, or exploiting you, that's never okay. You have rights and there is help.

Your Rights Under the Law:

  • RA 7610: Protection against child abuse[12]
  • RA 8353: Anti-Rape Law[13]
  • RA 11648: Age of sexual consent is 16[9]

  • Tell a trusted adult (parent, teacher, relative)
  • Go to a police station or barangay VAW desk
  • Seek medical attention if needed
  • Document incidents if safe to do so

Local Resources

Barangay VAW Desk

Every barangay should have a Violence Against Women (VAW) desk. This is your first point of contact for reporting abuse at the local level.

What to do: Go to your barangay hall and ask for the VAW desk.

Your barangay has a VAW desk. You can report there.

Local Health Center / Rural Health Unit

Your local health center provides basic health services, including prenatal check-ups, family planning counseling, and referrals.

What to do: Visit your nearest health center during operating hours.

Help is available in your community.

School Guidance Counselor

If you're a student, your school guidance counselor can provide counseling, referrals, and support.

What to do: Ask your school office about the guidance counseling services.

You Are Not Alone

Whatever you're going through, there are people and organizations that can help. Don't be afraid to reach out. Asking for help is a sign of strength, not weakness.
